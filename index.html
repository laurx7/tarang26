About

A decade of culture. A century of cinema.

तरंग
1913
Raja Harishchandra — India's first feature film. Silent era begins.
1931
Alam Ara — the voice arrives. Talkies transform Indian cinema forever.
1950s
The Golden Age. Guru Dutt, Bimal Roy, Raj Kapoor define a generation.
1975
Sholay — masala cinema peaks. Gabbar becomes immortal.
1990s
Dilwale. Romance era. DDLJ runs 25 years in a single theatre.
2000s
Lagaan. Dil Chahta Hai. New Wave. Parallel cinema finds its audience.
2022
RRR. KGF. Pushpa. Pan-India cinema redraws every border.
Now
OTT. 11 languages. A billion stories. The evolution continues.

Tarang is the annual cultural extravaganza of The PSBB Millennium School, Chennai — a festival that has been electrifying the city's school circuit for over a decade. What began as an intimate gathering of talent has grown into one of Chennai's most anticipated inter-school cultural competitions, drawing hundreds of participants from across the city each year.

Art is not what you see, but what you make others see — and at Tarang, we make Chennai see something extraordinary.

For Tarang '26, we turn the spotlight on the Evolution of Indian Cinema — a journey spanning more than a century, from the silent silhouettes of Raja Harishchandra to the thundering spectacle of RRR. Indian cinema isn't just an art form. It's a mirror held up to a billion lives, reflecting our languages, our conflicts, our joy, our grief, and our relentless hunger for story.

This year's fest is a love letter to that journey. Every event, every performance, every competition is infused with the spirit of cinema — its drama, its music, its arguments, its absurdities, its elegance. Whether you're on the court, the stage, or the debate floor, you're part of a story that's still being written.

Tarang is more than a competition. It's where friendships are formed under stage lights, where students discover voices they didn't know they had, and where schools across Chennai come together in the shared belief that culture is not a subject — it's a way of being.
